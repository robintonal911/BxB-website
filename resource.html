We are transforming 8600 de l’Épée, a former Bétonel paint factory across from Howard Park, into 31 social housing units with the support of the City of Montreal, ROMEL, and HAPOPEX. With a focus on family-friendly, affordable housing, most units will have at least two bedrooms. Supported by Comité d’action de Parc-Extension (CAPE), this project is the result of community mobilization and advocacy for accessible housing.

By reclaiming city-owned land for social housing, we are working to protect the right to housing in Parc-Extension and ensure long-term affordability for residents.

As a visual artist, the Resource Centre is where I bring my video projects to life. The media lab has everything I need—editing stations, cameras, mics, and software. I can connect with other people to share feedback, troubleshoot, or just offer a fresh perspective.
