Brique par Brique (BxB) is a nonprofit dedicated to creating community housing and cultural spaces that foster connection and social justice in Parc-Extension, one of Montreal’s most diverse neighborhoods.
↓|To address the urgent need for affordable, stable spaces for nonprofits and cultural organizations, BxB is developing a new community centre—a collaborative hub where diverse groups can share resources, ideas, and spaces to drive systemic change.
↓| By bringing together nonprofits, artists, and community organizations under one roof, this centre will strengthen collective action, reduce costs, and create a dynamic network of shared knowledge and support.
↓

Welcome to the Commons, the community living room and heart of Brique par Brique’s future community centre. The Commons is designed to be a warm, inviting, and flexible place where everyone feels at home. Whether you’re stopping by for a coffee, meeting up with a neighbor, or discovering a local pop-up event, this is your space to connect, collaborate, and feel a sense of belonging.

Metonymy Press is Brique by Brique’s current neighbour and future tenant of our new community centre! They publish literary fiction and nonfiction by emerging writers, publishing quality materials relevant to queer, feminist, and social justice communities.
